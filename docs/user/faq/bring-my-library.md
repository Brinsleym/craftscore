---
title: Can I bring my scores and markings from another app?
description: Your PDFs, yes. Markings, for some apps and formats.
---

## Answer

Your PDFs import as they are. ScoreCraft is planned to import:

- PDF annotations made in most PDF apps;
- forScore libraries exported as a `.4sc` file, with pages, ink, stamps and layers.

Exports from other score readers may lose markings if the app flattens them into the PDF or keeps
them in its own format.
